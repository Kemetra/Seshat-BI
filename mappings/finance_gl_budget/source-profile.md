## Why this is a SEPARATE source with its own profile

`finance_gl_budget` arrives at a **coarser grain** than `finance_gl_actuals` and is a
different business artifact: a plan, versioned, agreed ahead of the period -- not a record of
what was posted. It gets its own profile, its own map, its own declared grain and PK, and its
own readiness record. Nothing in this example merges the two into a single grain
(spec 137 SC-004).

## Grain and primary key (Phase 2.0, decided FIRST)

**One row = one budgeted amount for one account, in one department, for one fiscal quarter,
under one budget version.**

| Candidate key | Rows | Distinct | Unique? |
|---|---|---|---|
| (`fiscal_year`, `fiscal_quarter`, `account_code`, `department_code`) | 2,688 | 1,344 | **NO** |
| + `budget_version` (5 parts) | 2,688 | 2,688 | **YES** |

**`budget_version` is REQUIRED in the PK** -- this is measured, not asserted: without it the
key collides at exactly 2:1 (1,344 of 2,688). That is the data-side proof of FR-011: a
version is part of budget IDENTITY, so a new version ADDS rows and must never update,
overwrite, or supersede a prior version's rows.

**The summed figure is meaningless as a business number** and appears here only to show what
double-counting looks like: any query that does not filter to one `budget_version` sums the
plan twice. Every budget measure must pin its version.

**Baseline (OD-2, ruled by Ahmed Shaaban 2026-07-30): `ORIGINAL`.** Variance measures compare
actuals to `ORIGINAL`. `REVISION-1` remains in the source to exercise version identity and
the baseline-ambiguity variant (D10), but it never moves the headline Variance Amount or
Variance % measures.

## Additivity

`budget_amount` is **additive at and above its own grain** (sum across accounts, departments,
quarters within one version). It is **not** meaningfully divisible below quarter grain: no
monthly or line-level budget exists in the source, and OD-3 (ruled 2026-07-30) forbids
deriving one. A monthly-budget request is refused with the reason named -- variant D11.

Nothing semi-additive appears here. Balance-sheet snapshot grain is spec 091's scope and is
explicitly not exercised by this example.
