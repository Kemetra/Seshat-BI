## Provenance (and why this is not a found file)

Generated by `tests/fixtures/finance_gl/generate.py` from a single seeded PRNG
(`random.Random(20260730)`), with no clock, no UUID source, no network and no database.
Regeneration is byte-identical, verified by generating twice and comparing bytes AND
per-file SHA-256 in `tests/unit/test_finance_gl_generator.py::test_regeneration_is_byte_identical`.

Encoding is therefore **declared and tested**, not inferred: UTF-8 without BOM, comma
delimiter, header row present, `\n` line endings, fixed column order.

**RS1 still applies and is honoured.** Because this is a file source, `source_kind: "csv"`
is declared in `readiness-status.yaml` and Stage 1 stays **blocked** pending a named owner's
confirmation. The agent did not self-confirm it. The mismatch between "a human must confirm
an inferred encoding" and "a test proves a declared encoding" is recorded as ledger row L2 --
not resolved by carving out an exemption for our own fixture.

The full generated file is **git-ignored** (425,135 bytes -- roughly 236x the largest data
fixture this repo commits). Committed for citation: `tests/fixtures/finance_gl/excerpts/finance_gl_actuals.head.csv`.

## Grain and primary key (Phase 2.0, decided FIRST)

**One row = one journal line within one journal entry.**

| Candidate key | Rows | Distinct | Unique? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `journal_entry_id` alone | 5,000 | 2,500 | **NO** |
| (`journal_entry_id`, `line_id`) | 5,000 | 5,000 | **YES** |

The PK is therefore composite: **(`journal_entry_id`, `line_id`)** (RC2). Re-verify on the
transformed silver output.

This grain is DIFFERENT from `finance_gl_budget`'s (fiscal quarter x account x department x
budget version). The two are separate sources with separate maps and separate readiness
records; nothing merges them into one grain.

## Double-entry integrity

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Total debits | 31,135,795.20 |
| Total credits | 31,135,795.20 |
| Balanced | **YES** (equal to the cent) |
| Entries where debits != credits | 0 |
| Lines with both sides zero, or both non-zero | 0 |

**How balance is achieved in a P&L-only extract.** A P&L line cannot be balanced by another
P&L line -- the offsetting side of a real journal is normally a balance-sheet account, and
balance-sheet/snapshot grain is out of scope (spec 091). Each entry therefore pairs its P&L
line with one line on a dedicated **CLEARING** account (`1900`, `1910`;
`account_type = 'CLEARING'`). Exactly **2,500** clearing lines exist -- one per entry.

Excluding clearing accounts from P&L reporting is an explicit mapping decision recorded in
`source-map.yaml` and `assumptions.md`, NOT a filter applied silently in the generator.

## Coverage against the budget source (the grain-mismatch evidence)

Rolling actuals UP to the budget's comparison grain (fiscal year x quarter x account x
department):

| Measure | Count |
|---|---|
| Actual combos (all accounts) | 1,199 |
| Actual combos, **P&L accounts only** | 1,103 |
| Budget combos (per version) | 1,344 |
| P&L actual combos with **no** budget row | **0** |
| Actual combos with no budget row, **all account types** | 96 -- and all 96 are CLEARING |
| Budget combos with **no** P&L actuals | **241** |

Two facts a reader should take from this table:

1. The 96 unbudgeted actual combos are **exactly** the clearing accounts (2 accounts x 6
   departments x 8 quarters = 96). This is independent, data-side justification for the
   clearing exclusion decision: once clearing is excluded, actuals coverage against budget
   is complete.
2. The **241** budgeted combos with no posted activity are a **legitimate business
   exception**, not a data defect -- a department was budgeted for an account and posted
   nothing that quarter. They must surface in the report as an exception (the Missing Budget
   Flag metric's mirror case) and MUST NOT be reported as a mapping failure. Refusing them
   would be an over-refusal.

Aggregation direction is one-way: actuals may roll UP to quarter; budget is never spread
DOWN to month or line (OD-3, ruled 2026-07-30 -- no monthly derivation).
